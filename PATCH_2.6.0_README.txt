DataScope 2.6.0 - PATCH  (apply on top of 2.5.0)
================================================
Copy these files over your DataScope folder, keeping the same structure.
On 2.4.0 or earlier, use DataScope_v2.6.0_FULL.zip instead.

  core/html_report.py   per-series height, drawing order, keyboard fix
  core/models.py        version 2.6.0
  README.md, build_exe.bat, requirements.txt

1. PER-SERIES HEIGHT  (the x box on each chip in a graph's bar)
   Two channels on one graph can differ by a factor of a hundred - 気温 barely
   moves next to 日射量.  Set 気温 to x3 and its shape becomes readable.
   Two things it deliberately does NOT do:
     * it does not stretch the axis.  That would shrink every other series
       instead of enlarging the one you asked for, so the axis stays on the real
       data and the other lines do not move.
     * it does not multiply from zero.  A channel sitting at 60-100% of its own
       maximum would just leave the top of the frame.  It amplifies around the
       series' OWN MEAN, so the line grows in place; anything still off the edge
       is clipped rather than drawn over the labels.
   Any multiplier other than 1 is written as "x3" next to the name in the
   caption, the note, the readout heading and the legend of the exported image.
   The values in the readout and the CSV are always the real ones, and the
   setting is keyed by channel so it survives a saved view and a pruned export.

2. DRAWING ORDER  (the ◀ ▶ on each chip)
   Move a series along the bar to change which line is drawn on top - the
   rightmost chip is the front one.  Useful when a flat line hides under a
   bundle of others.

3. FIXED: THE ARROW KEYS STOPPED MOVING THE CURSOR
   After using the readout dropdown or a slider, that control kept the keyboard
   focus, so the arrow keys changed ITS value instead of walking the cursor
   along the chart.  Every control in those bars now hands the keyboard back
   when it is done, so the arrows always work - including in 常に表示 mode, as
   before.

Nothing was removed, and your profiles and session are kept.
